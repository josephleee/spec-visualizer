# Proposal: Retire paper loan cards

## Why

Since loans are recorded at the counter, the paper cards in each box are written twice and read never.

## What Changes

- Volunteers stop writing paper cards.
- A printed loan sheet replaces them during a power cut.
- The checkout requirement is renamed to match the words on the counter screen.

## Capabilities

### Modified Capabilities
- `loans`: removes paper loan cards; renames "Check out a tool" to "Lend a tool".

## Impact

- Counter procedure sheet; the box labels.
