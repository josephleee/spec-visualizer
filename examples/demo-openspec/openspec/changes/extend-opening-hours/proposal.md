# Proposal: Extend opening hours

## Why

The library opens on Saturday mornings only. Members who work weekends cannot borrow at all.

## What Changes

- A Wednesday evening opening, staffed by the new volunteers.
- Due dates that fall on a closed day move to the next opening.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities
- `opening-hours`: when the counter is open and how due dates follow it.

## Impact

- Due date calculation; the reminder job reads opening days.
