# Proposal: Add damage reports

## Why

Tools come back with a blunt blade or a missing bit, and the next member finds out at home.

## What Changes

- At return, the volunteer can record damage with a short note.
- A damaged tool leaves the shelf until a volunteer marks it repaired.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities
- `damage-reports`: recording damage at return and taking the tool out of use.

## Impact

- Return flow; catalog condition notes.
