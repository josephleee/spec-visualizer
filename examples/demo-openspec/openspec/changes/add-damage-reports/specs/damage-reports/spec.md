## Purpose
Keeping broken tools off the shelf until they are fixed.

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Report damage at return
The desk SHALL let the volunteer record damage with a note when a tool is returned.

#### Scenario: Blunt blade
- **WHEN** the volunteer returns a saw and records "blade blunt"
- **THEN** the saw is marked out of use with that note
- **AND** it no longer appears as on the shelf
